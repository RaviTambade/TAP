## ✨ Transflower Acceleration Program (TAP)

TAP is the training program that turns learning methodology into real-world capability.

### What TAP offers
- A structured learning experience guided by mentors and real projects.
- A blend of foundation, practice, reflection, and industry readiness.
- A progression from learning to building to becoming job-ready.

### How to use this section
- Start with the [Learning Methodology](../02_learning_methodology/README.md) to understand the philosophy behind the program.
- Move into [Roadmaps and Plans](../03_roadmaps_and_plans/README.md) to follow the training path.
- Use [Fullstack Stacks](../04_fullstack_stacks/README.md) and [Technology Essentials](../06_technology_essentials/README.md) for technical depth.

### Program focus
- Skill-first education
- Industry-aligned training
- Mentorship-driven learning
- Practice-based growth

This section is intended to be the entry point for students and learners who want to understand the Transflower training journey.
